**Answer to the research question: partly.** For this cohort, PCA reveals an interpretable environmental structure associated with bleaching severity, but it provides an incomplete summary of the observed bleaching-group differences. Across 23,069 events, 7 retained components explain 75.9% of environmental variance. PC4 and PC3 show the strongest pooled High-versus-Low contrasts (PC4: δ = +0.47; PC3: δ = +0.44). These associations should be read with the source-stratified comparisons and uncertainty audits, because component scores also vary with source and geography. The retained components preserve 50.5% of the sample-level squared Mahalanobis separation between High and Low relative to all original inputs. This is a descriptive separation ratio, not a measure of predictive accuracy. The findings support retaining original environmental indicators alongside the component summaries. They do not establish causal effects, operational warning thresholds, or independent future-year performance.
